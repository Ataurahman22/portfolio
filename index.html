Ajay Shrestha, PhD

Engineering Manager | AI Researcher | Educator

Introduction

Ajay Shrestha, PhD in Machine Learning (AI), is a seasoned engineering leader with nearly two decades of experience in technology and management. As an Engineering Manager at Imagen, a New York-based healthcare AI startup, he oversees the cloud infrastructure supporting the company’s AI-driven medical diagnostics-as-a-service platform.

Prior to this, Ajay led engineering teams at Thermo Fisher Scientific, building cloud-based solutions for life sciences. He is also an adjunct faculty member at the University of Bridgeport and serves on its Alumni Association Board.

Ajay is passionate about using technology to address critical societal challenges and is currently pursuing an MBA at Yale University.
